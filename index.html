Manager Library

A small library system to keep its book catalog, its members and its loans in one place, and to see which books are borrowed the most.

What it is

Manager Library replaces loose notebooks and spreadsheets with a single system. Librarians register loans and returns, administrators maintain the catalog and the members, and the team gets a report of the most borrowed titles at any time.

Out of scope on purpose: fines and payments, reservations, renewals, email or SMS, bulk import, loans between branches, and book attributes beyond title, author, ISBN, category and copies.

Actors

Administrator

Maintains the book catalog and the members, creates librarians and sees every report.

Signs in with username and password

Librarian

Registers loans and returns, and consults the catalog, the members and the overdue list.

Signs in with username and password

Member

The person who borrows books. Members do not log in: they are data in the system, not users.

No access to the system

Features

  1. 01

    Catalog

    Books with optional cover, five fixed categories, search by title or author, and soft delete.

  2. 02

    Members

    Register members by name and unique document, and search them with pagination.

  3. 03

    Loans

    One member, one to three books per loan. Each line takes a copy in the same operation, and a member can hold at most three open books.

  4. 04

    Returns

    Return each book line on its own. Returned copies go back to the shelf in the same operation.

  5. 05

    Overdue

    A list of loans past their due date, with the number of days late calculated from the dates.

  6. 06

    Report

    The most borrowed books, grouped by the title, ISBN and category they had when they were lent.

Five ideas behind the design

The architecture overview of the specification boils down to five rules. They are what keeps the data honest over time.

  1. A loan is an immutable fact

    Only the return date of a line changes, once.

  2. Copies move inside the same operation

    Creating or closing a loan line updates the available copies in the same transaction.

  3. State is calculated

    Open, overdue and closed are not stored in the database. They are derived from the dates.

  4. Frozen data does not follow the catalog

    Renaming a book today does not change last October's report.

  5. The database is the last barrier

    Constraints reject invalid copy counts even if something bypasses the application.
